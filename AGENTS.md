Identify the governing revision and relevant local changes. Honor a requested exact revision, resolve conflicting versions before implementation, and do not switch branches automatically. Unpublished local guidance does not establish public package availability or qualification.

This is the maintained public product source. Keep internal steering, installation-specific records, protected inputs and raw qualification output in private carriers, not this repository or its history. Publish selected artifact identities, outcomes and limits in the product's release/integration record. Historical private repositories are archives, not parallel development or documentation branches.
